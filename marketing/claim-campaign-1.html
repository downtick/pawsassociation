PAWS is the trade association for animal attractions: petting zoos, pony rides, beer burros, mobile farms, reptile shows and more. To help event planners find operators, we built a public directory, and your business may already be listed in it.

Here's why that matters for bookings:

  • Parents, schools, churches and party planners search the directory by attraction type, state and city.
  • Every listing has a Request availability button. On a claimed listing, that request goes straight to your inbox, with the planner's name, date and details.
  • On an unclaimed listing, the request can't reach you. Claiming connects it to you.

Claiming makes you a PAWS member ($149/year, with a 7-day money-back guarantee). Membership also adds your photos, services and service area to the listing, plus a 20-lesson marketing course, printable signs and business forms, and partner discounts.
